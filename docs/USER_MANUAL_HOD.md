# Moderated Learning Management System
## Head of Department User Manual

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Application:** Moderated Learning Management System (LMS)  
**Intended user:** Head of Department (HoD)

---

## 1. Introduction

This manual explains how the Head of Department (HoD) uses the Moderated Learning Management System to review lecturers' marks, moderate assessments, release results to students, and monitor academic assessment activities within the department.

The HoD is responsible for ensuring that marks are reviewed before they become visible to students.

## 2. Accessing the System

1. Open the LMS application on the school computer.
2. Enter your HoD username.
3. Enter your password.
4. Follow the displayed prompts to access the HoD menu.

Your account must have the Head of Department role.

## 3. HoD Menu

The HoD menu provides eight functions:

1. Review marked work awaiting moderation.
2. Approve a mark (optionally adjust it).
3. Return work to the lecturer for re-marking.
4. Release approved marks to students.
5. Reopen a released mark.
6. View all submissions in my department.
7. Moderated / released marks report.
8. View the audit trail of a submission.

Select the required option and follow the displayed prompts.

## 4. Reviewing Work Awaiting Moderation

1. Select **Review marked work awaiting moderation**.
2. The system displays submissions awaiting moderation within your department.
3. Review the available submission information.
